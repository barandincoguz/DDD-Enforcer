## Risks & mitigations

- **Risk:** SRS authoring eats too much time. **Mitigation:** Limit to 10 pages of essential domain content per SRS; quality matters more than length. Use existing public templates (e.g., IEEE 830) for structure.
- **Risk:** Generated codebase has subtle DDD compliance issues that contaminate RQ4 baseline. **Mitigation:** RQ4 explicitly seeds violations; the generator's tiny pre-existing leaks are picked up by Judge as TPs (this is fine — the recall floor measures what the framework finds, regardless of provenance).
- **Risk:** Public-domain D2/D3 SRSes carry redistribution restrictions. **Mitigation:** Default to authored SRSes; if a third-party SRS is borrowed, store only the bibliographic reference, not the file itself, and note in WP-12 replication package.
